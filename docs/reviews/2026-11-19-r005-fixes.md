## Confirmed finding

### G-1 (Low, gate) — writes after the transfer pass the whole gate when they sit in the payout arm
- **Where:** the transfer rules in `scripts/settlane_lint.py` look only inside the policy handler (`claim_handler`). The Claim arm that calls it was not checked.
- **Reproduction:** the Claim arm in `settlane.fe` became `let paid = handlers::claim_handler(id, m, to)`, then `milestones.set(key: (id, m), value: milestones.get(key: (id, m)))`, `totals.outstanding = totals.outstanding - Wei::zero()`, `paid`. fmt, check (both ingots), 70 tests, events, build and the lint with the ABI check all passed.
- **Impact:** none on the shipped bytecode. The stipend, which the lint enforces separately, stops re-entry. But SECURITY.md said writes before the send were enforced and that only the lint could see a write after a stipend send. That held only inside the handler. It is the R-005 b3 shape moved up one frame, and a Refund arm would have had the same gap.

## Remaining risks noted (not defects at `af68727`)
- **Provenance (R-2 in this record).** The lint did not check where `amount` and `to` came from. `let amount = amount + amount` or `let to = a.client` before the send passed the lint. The tests caught both (9 to 10 dev tests failed each time).
- **R-4 from R-005.** A `Refund` arm that lists `call` passed the text lint. Only the ABI lock (`--abi-dir out`) rejected it, because `refund(..)` was not in the allowlist.
- **Effect forwarding.** `with (C = call)` is rejected by the compiler (`8-0076`) and by `C-XFER-RAW` and `C-CLOCK`. A generic helper bound through an alias of `Call` passes the lint, but cannot be called (`docs/FE_NOTES.md`).
